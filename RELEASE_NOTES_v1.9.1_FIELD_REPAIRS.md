# PolyShield Asset IQ v1.9.1 Field Repair Build

Built from the v1.9 QR Tags release after mobile field testing.

## Repairs installed
- Edit Asset from the Asset Passport.
- Editing updates equipment metadata without exposing calculated health or inspection history for manual editing.
- Optional replacement cover photo while editing.
- QR / Tag shortcut beside the inspection controls.
- Separate Take Photo and Photo Library buttons during inspections.
- Talk-to-text buttons for overall notes, photo notes, and annotation notes.
- Browser speech-recognition fallback message directing iPhone users to the keyboard microphone when needed.
- Bottom navigation dynamically fits all role-appropriate tabs on one row.
- Asset evidence-library upload no longer forces camera-only capture.
- Version advanced to 1.9.1.

## Field acceptance test
1. Sign in on iPhone.
2. Open an asset and tap Edit asset.
3. Change a harmless field and save.
4. Verify health and historical inspections remain intact.
5. Start an inspection.
6. Test Talk to text.
7. Test Take photo.
8. Test Photo library.
9. Run AI analysis and apply/edit a draft.
10. Complete the inspection and confirm it appears in history.
11. Open QR / Tag and test Print QR tag / Order QR tag.
12. Confirm bottom navigation remains on one row.
