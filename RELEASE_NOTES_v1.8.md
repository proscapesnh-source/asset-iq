# PolyShield Asset IQ v1.8 — Soft Launch

## Launch focus
v1.8 prioritizes real organizational use and trustworthy data capture.

### New
- Supervisor dashboard for overdue inspections, open work and recent accountability events.
- Organization audit log for sign-ins and completed inspections, with role-restricted visibility.
- Customer-facing inspection reports no longer contain a separate AI-authored section. AI remains an internal drafting/analysis tool; the inspector owns the published report.
- Existing v1.7 PWA, Asset DNA, lifecycle, Knowledge Center, QR/passport, inspections and work orders remain intact.

### Product rule established
AI may assist the inspector internally. Published literature should present reviewed professional findings, not advertise AI authorship.

### Next technician track (does not block soft launch)
Component intelligence, pre-bid capture, geometry/surface-area calculators, measurement assistance, quote/scope assistance, code/spec prompts, and bid win/loss intelligence.
